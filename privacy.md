# PlantZen Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 September 2026**

PlantZen is a plant identification and plant care app for iPhone, made by WiseRock
("we", "us"), a business registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KVK 93580126, Hendrik-Ido-Ambacht).
This policy explains what data the app handles, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it and
what you can do about it.

The short version: we keep only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, we show no
ads, we run no tracking or analytics, and you can delete everything yourself from inside the app.

## 1. Who is responsible

WiseRock is the data controller for the personal data described here.

Contact: infowiserock@gmail.com

## 2. What we collect and why

### Your account

- **Sign in with Apple.** We receive a unique identifier from Apple. Your name and email address
  reach us only if you choose to share them on Apple's sign-in sheet; if you use Apple's "Hide My
  Email", we receive a relay address instead of your real one.
- **Guest account.** You can use PlantZen without signing in. A guest account is an anonymous
  identifier with no name and no email address. It belongs to that phone; if you delete the app
  without creating an account, the garden on it cannot be recovered.
- **Email sign-in.** If we offer sign-in by email code, we store the email address you enter.

Purpose: an account your garden belongs to. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.

### Photos you scan

When you identify a plant or check its health, the photo is uploaded to our storage and sent to
the identification service named in section 3. We keep the photo with its result as part of your
scan history and, if you add the plant to your garden, as that plant's photo.

Purpose: identification, diagnosis and your history. Legal basis: performance of our contract, and
your explicit consent for sending the photo to a third-party AI service, which the app asks for
once and which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Please photograph plants, not people. The app has no reason to receive a photo of a person.

### Your garden and care schedule

Plant names, nicknames, notes, care guides, watering and fertilising schedules, reminder settings,
completed care tasks and your health-check history.

Purpose: your garden and its reminders. Legal basis: performance of our contract. Reminders are
scheduled on your phone itself; nothing about them leaves your device.

### Botanist chat

The questions you type and the answers you receive. To answer, we send your question, the earlier
turns of that conversation and the saved details of the plant or scan you are asking about (its
name, its care guide or its diagnosis) to the AI provider named in section 3. Photos are never
sent to the chat provider.

Purpose: answering your questions. Legal basis: performance of our contract, and your explicit
consent for the third-party AI provider, the same consent as for photos.

Only your most recent conversations that are not attached to a plant are kept; the app tells you
how many. A conversation attached to a plant is kept with that plant.

### Purchases

Payments are made to Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your card or bank details. From
our subscription provider we receive the status of your subscription (which plan, whether it is
active or in a trial, when it renews or ends, and whether it is a test purchase), stored against
your account so the app knows what to unlock.

Purpose: providing what you paid for. Legal basis: performance of our contract, and our legal
obligations for accounting.

### Usage counters

How many identifications, health checks and Botanist questions you have used, so the free
allowance and the Premium fair-use limits can be applied. You can see these numbers yourself
under Settings › Usage.

### An install identifier

The app generates a random identifier on your phone and keeps it in the iOS keychain. It is sent
to our server together with a paid request (a scan or a question) so the free allowance is
counted per phone as well as per account. It is not an Apple identifier, it is shared with nobody
else and it is not used to track you.

Purpose: keeping the free allowance fair. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the
free tier sustainable.

### Setup answers and consent

The answers you give during setup (what you want from the app, roughly how many plants you have,
your experience, whether you want reminders) and the time you agreed to third-party AI processing.

### Technical data

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, time and type of request) for a
short period, for security and troubleshooting. Our own application logs are designed not to
contain personal data: no message text, no photos, no names, no email addresses.

### What we do not do

No advertising, no ad networks, no analytics kits, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale
or rental of personal data, no profiling for marketing.

## 4. How long we keep data

- **Your account, garden, photos and chats:** as long as your account exists.
- **Chats not attached to a plant:** only the most recent ones (the count is shown in the app);
  older ones are deleted automatically.
- **A deleted plant:** its reminders and the chats attached to it are deleted with it. Its scans
  stay in your scan history until you delete your account; email us if you want specific photos
  removed earlier.
- **Account deletion:** Settings › Delete account removes your account and everything linked to
  it from our systems at once, photos included, and we ask Kindwise to delete the identifications
  it holds for you. This cannot be undone. Copies held by our providers are removed on their own
  schedules; so are our hosting provider's server logs and routine backups.
- **A guest account you abandon** (deleting the app without deleting the account) keeps its data
  on our servers. Email us and we will remove it.
- **Purchase records** are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own rules; we keep the
  subscription status only while your account exists.

## 5. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you can ask us to give you access to your data,
correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, or hand it over in a portable form.
You can withdraw your consent to third-party AI processing at any time under Settings › Photo
analysis & AI; the app then asks again before the next scan or question. Deleting your account
and exporting your data are both available in Settings. For anything else, email
infowiserock@gmail.com; we answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens
([autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl)).

## 6. Children

PlantZen is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you
believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

## 7. Security

Data travels encrypted (TLS). Photos sit in a private storage bucket and are served only through
short-lived signed links. Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your phone. The keys to our
providers stay on our servers and are never in the app. Every database row is tied to its owner,
so one account can never read another's data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects
you, we will tell you as the law requires.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top tells you when. A change that matters to you is
announced in the app before it takes effect.
